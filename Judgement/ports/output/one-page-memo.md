# Output: One-page memo

## Reader and use

A partner, a manager, a colleague or a family member who needs the decision and the reasoning,
not the whole process. They read it in two minutes and either agree or push back.

## Length and register

250 to 350 words. Plain prose, short paragraphs, no tables. Written in the reader's language, not
the process's.

## Fields, in order

Decision, why (tied to outcome number one), what else was considered, what could go wrong and how
it is covered, first step and date, review date, what would change the decision.

## Template

```markdown
# {Decision in one line}

**Decision:** {what, by when, who does it}
**Why:** {the outcome it serves, in one or two sentences, and the strongest reason}
**What else I considered:** {the two or three real alternatives and the one-line reason each lost}
**What could go wrong:** {the one or two evidenced risks, and what covers each}
**First step:** {action, deadline}
**I will revisit this if:** {observable trigger}, **review on** {date}

*Full record: {where the decision journal entry lives}.*
```

## Rules for this output

- The never-drop fields that do not fit (decision quality, full probabilities, bias check) stay in
  the full record, and the memo names where it lives.
- A simulated board view, if included, gets one line and the word "simulated".

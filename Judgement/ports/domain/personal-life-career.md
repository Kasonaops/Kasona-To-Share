# Domain pack: personal life and career

## Fits

A job change, quitting, accepting or declining an offer, a move, a course of study, going
self-employed, a sabbatical, a living-situation or relationship call. It does not fit a decision
that mainly concerns a business's product or price (use the business-and-product pack) and it is
the wrong tool for acute distress (see hard limits).

## Horizons

| Kind of decision | Pre-mortem horizon | Default review |
|---|---|---|
| Job change, accepting or declining an offer | 12 to 18 months | 6 months in, then at 12 |
| Move, course of study, going self-employed | 24 to 36 months | 12 months in, then at 24 |
| A reversible trial (a project, a side role) | 3 to 6 months | At the end of the trial |

## Option types to remember

- The status quo, with its own consequences. Staying is a decision, not an absence of one.
- Combinations: part-time, a trial period, starting something in parallel.
- The uncomfortable option you would rather not write down. At least one of the options must
  be one you dislike.
- A smaller first step: a conversation, a shadow day, a short contract, a sublet.

## MECE buckets for sorting options

- **By how reversible it is:** trial, staged commitment, full commitment, status quo.
- **By what it changes first:** where you work, what you work on, who you work with, what you earn.
  Pick one distinguishing question per sort and stay with it.

## How the biases show up here

| Bias | How it shows up |
|---|---|
| Sunk cost | Years in training, in a job, in a city. The time already spent is gone either way and does not get a vote on the future |
| Confirmation bias | Asking exactly the people you know will agree with you |
| Identity bias | "I am not an employee", "I am not someone who gives up". A statement about self-image, not about the option |
| Social desirability | The title, what family or former colleagues would think, how it sounds told as a story. Ask: how would I decide if nobody found out? |
| Anchoring on one number | Salary, a grade, an age, a year. The measurable number crowds out everything that is not |
| Loss aversion | A safe, known state defended against an equally valuable uncertain one, because one is a loss and the other a gain |
| Status quo bias | Staying because staying needs no action |

## Consequences that get overlooked

- Consequences for other people affected, written out (a partner, a team four weeks before a
  launch, children, a landlord).
- The cost of the decision date passing without a decision.
- What you are not doing while you do this, and what that costs over time.
- Sleep: does an option cost sleep on an ongoing basis? That is a legitimate criterion.

## Hard limits

| Topic | The engine can | The engine cannot, and who to ask |
|---|---|---|
| Legal (employment contract, notice period, severance, custody, inheritance, visa) | Structure the decision, flag open legal questions as must-know | No legal advice and no reading of clauses or deadlines. A qualified lawyer for the relevant area |
| Medical (diagnosis, treatment, capacity to cope) | Structure the decision around the medical information you give | No medical assessment. A doctor |
| Acute psychological distress (exhaustion, ongoing low mood, crisis, thoughts of self-harm) | Nothing. This process is the wrong tool | Therapeutic or medical help. In an acute crisis, immediate professional help, not a journal entry |
| Tax and financial (severance, self-employment, retirement) | Raise the question, flag it as a gap | No tax or investment advice. A tax advisor, and a licensed advisor for any actual investment |
| Conflict with other people involved | Clarify your own goals and options | Does not replace a real conversation with the other person |

## Translation notes

| Term | What it means here |
|---|---|
| Option | A concrete path you would commit time and energy to |
| Assumption | A belief about your own future, for example "this role gets me toward X in two years" |
| Assumption drift | The belief that got you here no longer holds and you have not said so out loud |
| Concentration | Everything riding on one path, one employer, one city, one relationship |
| Position sizing | How big a first step to take: a trial instead of a jump |
| Exit trigger | What would have to happen for me to revisit this decision |
| Circle of competence | Can I explain what I am getting into in two plain sentences, without jargon |

## What does not translate

There is no continuously observable price for a life path, no independent "fair value" of an
option, no benchmark for a life well lived, and no audited data. The quality of the process
therefore rests on the probability step: estimates must be presented as estimates, with a stated
basis, never as facts. Comparing yourself to other people is not a benchmark, it is the social
desirability bias.
